# 0010: The first minutes

**Status**: accepted, 2026-10-28 (decided by Claude on the owner's delegation).

**Context**: a new player waited 60-120 s before a first fight (title, consent, a 6-page intro,
Kagari, finding the Vigil-Keeper in the temple, the Well, difficulty, the descent), was hit
standing still in 7 starts out of 33, and met fast monsters first.

**Decision**:
- On a profile with no run yet, after the privacy choice and the story (skippable), **the first
  descent starts directly** on floor 1 at Keeper, without Kagari or the difficulty choice; Kagari,
  the Vigil-Keeper and the choices come at the first return, with her first-death line.
- On floor 1 of a first run, monsters ignore the hero while he is in the starting room until he
  has both moved and attacked; the first room next to it holds a slow, clearly telegraphed monster
  that dies in 2-3 hits of the starting weapon.
- The death screen offers **Descend again** (straight back to the Well's descent with the same
  difficulty) besides returning to the village.

**Consequences**: a test measures launch-to-first-fight for a new profile (target under 30 s
when the story is skipped).
